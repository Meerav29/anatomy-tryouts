### 2026: active aero
- DRS scrapped after 15 years; front and rear wings move actively. Original FIA names (shown at Canadian GP presentation): X-mode (low drag, straights), Z-mode (high downforce, corners), Manual Override Mode (MOM). Interim: Straight Line mode / Cornering mode. Final: "Straight mode" / "Corner mode", "Overtake Mode" (replaced Manual Override Mode), "Boost Mode", "Recharge". Sources: https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/new-f1-2026-mode-names-and-car-images-revealed/ ; https://www.crash.net/f1/news/1050188/1/manuel-override-mode-f1s-new-overtaking-aid-and-drs-replacement-explained ; https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-unveils-formula-1-regulations-for-2026-and-beyond-featuring-more-agile.75qJiYOHXgeJqsVQtDr2UB
- Overtake Mode: available within 1 second of the car ahead; extra electrical energy. Unlike DRS, straight mode is available at all times irrespective of gaps. Power figures conflict: 350kW to 337 km/h vs up to 355 km/h/340 km/h (see section 7).
- Date agreed: the WMSC ratified the 2026 power unit regs on 28 June (year not confirmed in my results; widely reported as 2024 but UNVERIFIED). Original unveiling at Canadian GP "last year" per one source, year UNVERIFIED. Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-world-motor-sport-council-approves-power-unit-regulations-for.495Jt6I1pAAsytVVYjz2Z5

## 6. WHY IT CHANGED (timeline)
- 2007-09: Overtaking Working Group; 2009 regs add narrower/higher rear, wider front wing, driver-adjustable front flap (regulation).
- 2010: McLaren F-duct stalls the rear wing (team innovation); Abu Dhabi 2010 Alonso-Petrov train feeds the overtaking debate.
- 2010 mid-year: teams vote to ban F-duct (regulation by team vote); 2011: front-wing adjuster out, DRS in (regulation).
- 2011: DRS 50mm slot, 1s rule, 1-2 zones; overtakes per race jump 23.8 to 43.2 (Autosport).
- 2012: Mercedes double DRS (innovation); banned 2013 by majority vote (regulation).
- 2012-15: overtakes per race fall 35 to 24 (RaceFans), as tyre effects fade.
- 2016-17: Pirelli count 866 then 435 as 2017 wide-car aero makes following harder (regulation-driven).
- 2019: wider/taller rear wing, 65 to 85mm (regulation).
- 2020: Mercedes DAS (innovation), banned 2021.
- 2022: ground-effect cars; DRS retained as precaution; Pirelli passes 599 to 785.
- 2023: Red Bull DRS "mega big" effect (innovation); Melbourne four zones.
- 2025: flex-wing and mini-DRS clampdown (regulation); last DRS year.
- 2026: DRS replaced by active aero (Straight/Corner) and Overtake Mode (regulation).
